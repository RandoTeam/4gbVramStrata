Create a single-file browser project called "Underwater Observation Pod Explorer".

The page should show a stylized 3D first-person scene inside a small underwater observation pod. The user should be able to move around inside the pod and look around freely.

Requirements:
Use Three.js.
Everything must be in one index.html file.
The project should run immediately in a modern browser.

Camera and movement:
Use a first-person viewpoint.
Allow the user to move only inside the pod interior.
Movement should support desktop and touch devices.
Desktop controls:
- WASD or arrow keys to move,
- mouse drag or pointer look to look around.
Touch controls:
- on-screen joystick or directional pad for movement,
- on-screen look control or touch drag for looking around.
Add visible on-screen controls for touch that stay compact and readable.
Movement should include collision handling.
The camera should not pass through walls, windows, consoles, seats, lockers, pipes, or other large objects.
The player should stay within the room and feel grounded at a believable eye height.

Scene:
The pod should be a small walkable underwater observation room with a clear interior layout.
The interior should include:
- curved glass viewing windows,
- a metal floor,
- wall or ceiling pipes,
- cables,
- control consoles,
- blinking screens,
- gauges,
- warning lights,
- storage lockers,
- seats,
- railings or structural supports,
- a hatch or door area,
- and several small props that make the room feel believable.

Outside the pod there should be a rich underwater world with:
- rocks,
- coral,
- sea plants,
- schools of fish,
- floating particles,
- bubbles,
- and at least one larger distant creature or a passing submarine.

The underwater environment should be visible in multiple directions, not only in front of the user.
The outside world should feel deep and layered, with clear foreground, midground, and background.

Atmosphere and animation:
Add underwater fog.
Add light rays filtering through water.
Add caustic light patterns.
Add soft reflections or highlights on the glass.
Add subtle animation in sea plants, fish, particles, bubbles, and distant creatures.
The pod interior should also feel alive:
- small screen animations,
- blinking lights,
- subtle ambient motion,
- slight changes in light from the water outside.

Visual quality:
The interior should feel dense, polished, and rewarding to inspect while moving around.
The exterior should feel immersive and visually rich.
Objects should be grounded and placed with believable scale.
The scene should feel like one coherent underwater environment rather than separate disconnected decorations.

UI:
Add a compact UI with:
- Reset,
- Pause,
- Quality.
The UI should stay small and should not cover the main view.

Evaluation focus:
Focus on visual atmosphere, depth, composition, lighting, believable first-person exploration, readable touch controls, and reliable collision-based movement inside the pod.
