# Support 4gbVramStrata

> **This project is created, developed and measured by a single engineer**, on one laptop, with one 4 GB card.

Every number in this repository was measured on a GeForce RTX 3050 Laptop with 4 GB of VRAM: an engine, an
installer, a server, twelve models' worth of compatibility work, and every speed figure with its log. There is no
cloud bill, no API key, and nothing here is a paid tier — the whole point is that the hardware you already own is
enough.

If this project runs a 35B model on your 4 GB card, saves you an upgrade you were about to buy, or is simply useful
in your work, a contribution pays for the one thing that makes the numbers trustworthy: **time on the reference
laptop, disks for more model files, and the electricity of measuring instead of guessing.**

## What a contribution actually buys

Said plainly, because "support development" means nothing on its own:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Measurement time** | A speed claim needs eight runs on a quiet machine with the power profile set, and a quality claim needs 22 prompts twice. That is hours of a card at full load, and the electricity is real. |
| **Model files** | Every "runs here" row is a 12 to 24 GB download and a place to keep it. Ornith 1.5, Qwen3.6 and Tiel Coder are downloaded, tested and then usually deleted again. |
| **A second card** | The most useful thing this project could add is measurements on **6 GB and 8 GB cards**, to say exactly where the 4 GB advice stops applying. |
| **Nothing else** | No salaries, no office, no cloud. |

What it does **not** buy: priority, private builds, a paid tier, or influence over a measurement. A number here changes
when a run says so, and not before.
